# Storyteller's notes: Sects & Violets

Compiled for the Storyteller judge from the Sects & Violets almanac's How to Run sections and examples, the
rulebook (madness, alignment and character) and the wiki, in our own words. One section per character: what the
Storyteller decides for it, the interactions to watch on this script, and how a good Storyteller handles them. In
Sects & Violets the town must first work out which Demon is in play; characters and alignments change, Minions act
visibly, and information is often false. Never say why something happened. The judge reads the sections for the
characters in play.

## Witch
Each night the Witch curses a player; if that player nominates tomorrow, they die at once, and their nomination still
goes to a vote. The curse lasts one day; the same player can be cursed again. Once only three players are alive the
Witch loses the ability at once, a curse already made included. A drunk or poisoned Witch's curse is wasted; a sober
Witch's curse pauses while the Witch is drunk and comes back when they sober up. A curse follows the player through
a character change (a cursed Klutz who becomes the Fang Gu dies nominating, and good wins). A Witch killed by the
Vigormortis keeps cursing. Nothing for you to decide; the visible deaths tell the town a Witch is about.

## Cerenovus
Each night the Cerenovus picks a player (alive or dead) and a good character; that player is told, and must spend
tomorrow trying to convince the group they are that character. Judge madness like humour: by effort and intent, not
by a word test. A sincere claim, defended, with the character's "information" made up, keeps them safe, even if
nobody believes it; saying "no" when asked whether they are mad is right. Silence, one token line, a hint or wink, a
private confession you overhear, claiming something else, or saying "I am mad" breaks it. A player who breaks it
might be executed: usually yes for a good player (it is their choice, even if it ends the game: a mad good twin
executed loses it for good), more sparingly for an evil player, so evil can't use a madness execution to end the day
before a dangerous nomination. A madness execution is the day's one execution, and it counts against a Vortox. A
drunk or poisoned Cerenovus makes nobody mad. The Cerenovus rarely picks the Demon.

## Pit-Hag
Each night but the first, the Pit-Hag picks a player and a character on the script; if no one has that character
(a dead player's counts), the player becomes it and is told, keeping their alignment (a good Witch, an evil
Townsfolk). The new character's ability is fresh. If a Demon is made, deaths tonight are yours to choose, as the
Pit-Hag's attacks: a Demon turned into another Demon, it is best that nobody dies tonight, so the town sees the
change (you may even cancel the Demon's attack); a new evil Demon, kill the old Demon (the new one still attacks);
a good Demon, kill the evil Demon if it is the final night, otherwise let both run, remembering all Demons must die
for good to win. Turning the Demon into a non-Demon ends the game for good unless an Evil Twin holds it. A new
Evil Twin is paired at once with a player of the other alignment, and both learn each other. A Pit-Hag killed by
the Vigormortis keeps acting until the Vigormortis loses its ability.

## Evil Twin
At setup you pick the twin: any good player. On the first night both learn each other: the twin learns who the Evil
Twin is, the Evil Twin the twin's character. Executing the good one of the two (whichever character they have) wins
for evil while the Evil Twin has their ability; executing the evil one does nothing special; good cannot win while
both live, so a dead Demon with both twins alive means the game goes on, with no more night deaths. A dead Evil Twin
has no ability (unless the Vigormortis killed them). If the twins end up on the same side (a Fang Gu jump into the
twin), pick a new twin of the other alignment and tell both. A new Evil Twin on the final night should be paired with
the Demon or a dead player, so good can still win.

## Fang Gu
Each night but the first, the Fang Gu kills. The first Outsider it would kill instead becomes an evil Fang Gu and the
old Fang Gu dies; this happens once a game, whatever happens later. The new Fang Gu learns their character and that
they are evil, but not who the Minions are. If the Outsider would not die (dead already, protected), nothing jumps
and the Fang Gu lives. One extra Outsider is dealt. A jumped Outsider does not trigger their own death ability
(they did not die). Many Outsider claims hint at a Fang Gu; a Demon dying at night does too.

## Vigormortis
Each night but the first, the Vigormortis kills. A Minion it kills keeps their ability while dead and poisons one
Townsfolk neighbour, the nearest Townsfolk one way or the other round the circle (alive or dead), your choice: often
next to a troublesome Townsfolk. All of it holds only while the Vigormortis has its ability: if it dies, is drunk or
poisoned, or becomes something else, the dead Minions lose their abilities and the poison ends. A dead Minion who
becomes a non-Minion, or is drunk or poisoned, has no ability. One Outsider fewer is dealt (none removed if there are
none). Dead Minions still acting (a curse, madness, a transformation) are its fingerprint.

## No Dashii
Each night but the first, the No Dashii kills. The nearest Townsfolk each way round the circle, alive or dead, are
poisoned from the start (Outsiders and Minions are skipped), and the poison moves at once when seats change character
(a new No Dashii, a poisoned Townsfolk who becomes an Outsider) and ends if the No Dashii loses its ability. Its two
neighbours' information should mislead, in ways that fit what the evil players claim. Townsfolk who keep getting odd
information point to a No Dashii beside them.

## Vortox
Each night but the first, the Vortox kills. While it has its ability, every Townsfolk's information from their
ability is false, sober or not; choose false answers that mislead rather than ones the town can simply reverse, and
support evil's bluffs. A character or alignment change is not information and is always true; Outsiders' and
Minions' information is unaffected. At each dusk, if nobody was executed today (an execution of a dead player, or a
madness execution, counts), evil wins.
