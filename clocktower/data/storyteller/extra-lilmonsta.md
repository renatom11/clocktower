# Storyteller's notes: Lil' Monsta (batch 6, removable)

## Lil' Monsta
No player holds the Demon here: the Minions pass a token between themselves, and whoever has it at the moment is the
Demon for every purpose (dying as it, registering as it, the game's end) while keeping their own Minion ability. Each
night the Minions point at one of themselves; if they point at different players, settle it (keeping the current
babysitter is the quiet choice, moving it creates a story). Its kill is yours to make: most nights a good player should
die, as a Demon's would, so the town feels the pressure; skip a night only when a death would decide the game too
early. Never kill the babysitter yourself. Information about the babysitter may show either Lil' Monsta or their
Minion character; the Fortune Teller always sees a Demon there, and a Slayer's shot always lands.
