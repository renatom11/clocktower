# Storyteller's notes: the Wizard (batch 6, removable)

## Wizard
The Wizard gets one wish, picked from a short list you can actually deliver. Grant most of them: a refused wish is a
wasted Minion. The price should sting in proportion to the wish (a death wish deserves a real cost, a peek at the
bluffs a small one), and should give good something to work with without naming the Wizard outright every time. A clue
is optional; an announcement at dawn tells the town that something unusual happened without saying what, while a
private hint to one good player can start a useful argument. If the Wizard words a wish freely, match it to the closest
item on the list or treat it as no wish; never grant something the list does not hold.
