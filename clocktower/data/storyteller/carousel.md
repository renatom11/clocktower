# Storyteller's notes: the experimental characters

Written for the Storyteller judge from the wiki's How to Run sections, examples and jinx texts for the experimental
characters, in our own words. One section per character: what you decide for it, what to watch for, and how a good
Storyteller handles it on a custom script. Jinxes listed on the script's sheet are in force whenever both characters
are on it. Never say why something happened. The judge reads the sections for the characters in play.

## Banshee
Nothing to decide while she lives. When the Demon's attack kills a sober, healthy Banshee, you tell the whole town at
dawn that the Banshee has died, and from then on she may nominate two players a day and raise two hands on every
vote, though dead, without using her ghost vote. A Banshee who was drunk or poisoned as she died gets nothing, and
neither does one executed or killed some other way. With the Vortox on the script, its jinx makes you announce her
anyway (the announcement is not false information).

## Damsel
At the start every Minion learns that a Damsel is in play, not who; if one is made later, the Minions learn it then.
Each Minion may publicly guess the Damsel once, by name: a right guess loses the game for the Damsel's team, at once.
A guess from a drunk or poisoned Minion, or at a drunk or poisoned Damsel, does nothing (it is still spent). With the
Spy on the script, a Damsel is poisoned for good once a Spy has been in play, so guesses do nothing. When a Pit-Hag
creates a Damsel you pick who becomes her: usually not the player the Pit-Hag named, so evil cannot just guess.

## Steward
On the first night the Steward learns one good player. Pick someone useful but not decisive; a Spy may be shown as
good, a Recluse should not be. A drunk or poisoned Steward may be shown an evil player, ideally one bluffing a good
character, which makes the lie believable. Under the Vortox the player shown must be evil.

## Nightwatchman
Once per game, on any night, the Nightwatchman may pick a player, who is woken and shown who the Nightwatchman is.
It is a trust tool: a sober one's target learns the truth. If the Nightwatchman is drunk or poisoned (or the Vortox
is in play) the target may be shown someone else, or simply not woken; either way the ability is gone.

## Magician
Only the first night's evil information changes. The Demon sees the Magician among its Minions; the Minions are
shown both the Magician and the Demon as their Demon, not knowing which is real. Nothing about registration
changes: abilities that detect the Demon still find the real one. A Magician poisoned from the start (next to a No
Dashii) does nothing. With no Demon in play (a Summoner) the Minions are shown the Magician alone. With the Spy on
the script, the Demon's and the Magician's tokens are hidden whenever the Spy looks at the Grimoire.

## Fisherman
Once per game the Fisherman visits you by day for advice that helps their team win. Give one true statement, drawn
from the Grimoire, that points their team the right way: for a good Fisherman something that narrows down evil (who
is evil, how near the Demon sits, who is poisoned, a Minion's character), short of naming the Demon outright; for an
evil Fisherman, something about the good characters or the setup. A drunk or poisoned Fisherman may get bad advice,
a false statement; under the Vortox the advice must be false. The engine lists the statements it can check; rank them.

## Golem
The Golem nominates once per game, ever. If the player they nominate is not the Demon, that player dies at once,
and the vote still goes ahead. A Recluse may register as the Demon and live: use it sparingly. A drunk or poisoned
Golem kills nobody, and has still used their nomination.

## Politician
At the end, if the Politician's team lost and they were the player most responsible for it, they change alignment
and win too, even if dead. The engine counts only the plainest case: the game ended on today's execution and the
Politician was the player executed or the one who nominated them. A drunk or poisoned Politician gets nothing. An evil
player the Pit-Hag turned into the Politician cannot turn good this way.

## Hatter
When the Hatter dies (sober and healthy), that night at the Hatter's step each living Minion and Demon may become
another character of their own type on the script that no other player has, or stay as they are. They are told
their new character. With the Summoner on the script, a Summoner who makes a second living Demon makes deaths that
night yours to choose.

## Plague Doctor
When a sober Plague Doctor dies, you gain the ability of a Minion on the script that is not in play, once. Pick one
that keeps the game close: a nightly poison or curse, a Devil's Advocate's protection, a single assassination, a
Godfather's revenge, a madness, or the Mastermind's extra day. Jinxes change four: the Spy's and the Scarlet
Woman's go to a living Minion, who learns it; the Baron's turns up to two Townsfolk into Outsiders; the Evil Twin's
makes a player the Evil Twin. You never take the Pit-Hag's or the Summoner's. Use your ability where it helps the
team that is behind, and never to end the game by your own choice.

## Summoner
No Demon is dealt; a Townsfolk takes its place, and the Summoner learns three good characters not in play. On the
third night the Summoner picks any player, alive or dead, and a Demon on the script: that player becomes that Demon
and evil, and is told. Until then the game does not end for want of a Demon; if the Summoner dies first, or is drunk
or poisoned on that night with no jinx to help, no Demon comes and good wins. Jinxes: with the Courtier, you summon
in place of a living Summoner who has no ability (usually what they chose); with the Pukka, a Pukka may come on the
second night instead; a dead player summoned into a Zombuul has already died once; with the Pit-Hag or the Hatter,
a second living Demon makes tonight's deaths yours; the Clockmaker counts the Summoner as the Demon.

## Ojo
Each night but the first the Ojo names a character, and that character's player dies. If no one has that character,
you choose who dies: normally a good player who will actually die, rarely nobody. Protections still work (a Soldier
named survives). A drunk or poisoned Ojo kills nobody.

## Al-Hadikhia
Each night but the first the Al-Hadikhia may pick three players; at dawn you announce who. Each is woken in turn and
silently chooses to live or die: those who choose to die die, but if all three choose to live, all three die. A dead
player who chooses to live comes back. A drunk or poisoned Al-Hadikhia's targets still choose, to no effect. With the
Mastermind, an executed Al-Hadikhia (Mastermind alive) picks three good players that night: if all three choose to
live, evil wins, otherwise good. With the Scarlet Woman, a dead Demon brought back while she holds the Demon makes her
the Scarlet Woman again.
