# Storyteller's notes: the experimental characters

Written for the Storyteller judge from the wiki's How to Run sections, examples and jinx texts for the experimental
characters, in our own words. One section per character: what you decide for it, what to watch for, and how a good
Storyteller handles it on a custom script. Jinxes listed on the script's sheet are in force whenever both characters
are on it. Never say why something happened. The judge reads the sections for the characters in play.

## Banshee
Nothing to decide while she lives. When the Demon's attack kills a sober, healthy Banshee, you tell the whole town at
dawn that the Banshee has died, and from then on she may nominate two players a day and raise two hands on every
vote, though dead, without using her ghost vote. A Banshee who was drunk or poisoned as she died gets nothing, and
neither does one executed or killed some other way. With the Vortox on the script, its jinx makes you announce her
anyway (the announcement is not false information).

## Damsel
At the start every Minion learns that a Damsel is in play, not who; if one is made later, the Minions learn it then.
Each Minion may publicly guess the Damsel once, by name: a right guess loses the game for the Damsel's team, at once.
A guess from a drunk or poisoned Minion, or at a drunk or poisoned Damsel, does nothing (it is still spent). With the
Spy on the script, a Damsel is poisoned for good once a Spy has been in play, so guesses do nothing. When a Pit-Hag
creates a Damsel you pick who becomes her: usually not the player the Pit-Hag named, so evil cannot just guess.

## Steward
On the first night the Steward learns one good player. Pick someone useful but not decisive; a Spy may be shown as
good, a Recluse should not be. A drunk or poisoned Steward may be shown an evil player, ideally one bluffing a good
character, which makes the lie believable. Under the Vortox the player shown must be evil.

## Nightwatchman
Once per game, on any night, the Nightwatchman may pick a player, who is woken and shown who the Nightwatchman is.
It is a trust tool: a sober one's target learns the truth. If the Nightwatchman is drunk or poisoned (or the Vortox
is in play) the target may be shown someone else, or simply not woken; either way the ability is gone.

## Magician
Only the first night's evil information changes. The Demon sees the Magician among its Minions; the Minions are
shown both the Magician and the Demon as their Demon, not knowing which is real. Nothing about registration
changes: abilities that detect the Demon still find the real one. A Magician poisoned from the start (next to a No
Dashii) does nothing. With no Demon in play (a Summoner) the Minions are shown the Magician alone. With the Spy on
the script, the Demon's and the Magician's tokens are hidden whenever the Spy looks at the Grimoire.

## Fisherman
Once per game the Fisherman visits you by day for advice that helps their team win. Give one true statement, drawn
from the Grimoire, that points their team the right way: for a good Fisherman something that narrows down evil (who
is evil, how near the Demon sits, who is poisoned, a Minion's character), short of naming the Demon outright; for an
evil Fisherman, something about the good characters or the setup. A drunk or poisoned Fisherman may get bad advice,
a false statement; under the Vortox the advice must be false. The engine lists the statements it can check; rank them.

## Golem
The Golem nominates once per game, ever. If the player they nominate is not the Demon, that player dies at once,
and the vote still goes ahead. A Recluse may register as the Demon and live: use it sparingly. A drunk or poisoned
Golem kills nobody, and has still used their nomination.

## Politician
At the end, if the Politician's team lost and they were the player most responsible for it, they change alignment
and win too, even if dead. The engine counts only the plainest case: the game ended on today's execution and the
Politician was the player executed or the one who nominated them. A drunk or poisoned Politician gets nothing. An evil
player the Pit-Hag turned into the Politician cannot turn good this way.

## Hatter
When the Hatter dies (sober and healthy), that night at the Hatter's step each living Minion and Demon may become
another character of their own type on the script that no other player has, or stay as they are. They are told
their new character. With the Summoner on the script, a Summoner who makes a second living Demon makes deaths that
night yours to choose.

## Plague Doctor
When a sober Plague Doctor dies, you gain the ability of a Minion on the script that is not in play, once. Pick one
that keeps the game close: a nightly poison or curse, a Devil's Advocate's protection, a single assassination, a
Godfather's revenge, a madness, or the Mastermind's extra day. Jinxes change four: the Spy's and the Scarlet
Woman's go to a living Minion, who learns it; the Baron's turns up to two Townsfolk into Outsiders; the Evil Twin's
makes a player the Evil Twin. You never take the Pit-Hag's or the Summoner's. Use your ability where it helps the
team that is behind, and never to end the game by your own choice.

## Summoner
No Demon is dealt; a Townsfolk takes its place, and the Summoner learns three good characters not in play. On the
third night the Summoner picks any player, alive or dead, and a Demon on the script: that player becomes that Demon
and evil, and is told. Until then the game does not end for want of a Demon; if the Summoner dies first, or is drunk
or poisoned on that night with no jinx to help, no Demon comes and good wins. Jinxes: with the Courtier, you summon
in place of a living Summoner who has no ability (usually what they chose); with the Pukka, a Pukka may come on the
second night instead; a dead player summoned into a Zombuul has already died once; with the Pit-Hag or the Hatter,
a second living Demon makes tonight's deaths yours; the Clockmaker counts the Summoner as the Demon.

## Ojo
Each night but the first the Ojo names a character, and that character's player dies. If no one has that character,
you choose who dies: normally a good player who will actually die, rarely nobody. Protections still work (a Soldier
named survives). A drunk or poisoned Ojo kills nobody.

## Al-Hadikhia
Each night but the first the Al-Hadikhia may pick three players; at dawn you announce who. Each is woken in turn and
silently chooses to live or die: those who choose to die die, but if all three choose to live, all three die. A dead
player who chooses to live comes back. A drunk or poisoned Al-Hadikhia's targets still choose, to no effect. With the
Mastermind, an executed Al-Hadikhia (Mastermind alive) picks three good players that night: if all three choose to
live, evil wins, otherwise good. With the Scarlet Woman, a dead Demon brought back while she holds the Demon makes her
the Scarlet Woman again.

## Pixie
On the first night the Pixie is shown one Townsfolk character that someone really has, and must then act as if
they were that character, convincingly, all game. If the player who has it dies while the Pixie is alive, sober
and has kept up the act, the Pixie gains that ability for good. Judge the act on the whole game: a Pixie who never
claimed it, denied it or confessed gets nothing. A drunk or poisoned Pixie, or one under the Vortox, is shown a
Townsfolk nobody has.

## High Priestess
Each night you point the High Priestess at the one player you think they should spend their time talking to. For a
good one, pick a living good player whose information or reasoning would help them most, ideally someone they have
not spoken with much; never use it to name the Demon outright. A drunk or poisoned High Priestess may be sent to
anyone, often an evil player; under the Vortox it must be someone they should not trust.

## Poppy Grower
While the Poppy Grower lives and is sober, the Minions and Demon start without knowing each other: the Demon still
gets three bluffs. Once the Poppy Grower dies (or loses the ability), evil learns who is who that night. A drunk
player who only thinks they are the Poppy Grower changes nothing. Jinxes: no Grimoire for the Spy or the Widow while
the Poppy Grower has the ability; with the Summoner, you pick which player becomes the Demon the Summoner names.

## Shugenja
On the first night the Shugenja learns whether the nearest evil player sits clockwise or anticlockwise, counting
seats. When two evil players are the same distance either way, pick freely. A Spy may count as good and a Recluse
as evil here. A drunk or poisoned Shugenja is pointed the wrong way, as is one under the Vortox.

## Balloonist
Each night show the Balloonist one other player, whose character type must differ from the type you showed the
night before (a Spy or Recluse may count as whatever they can register as). On the first night anyone will do. A
drunk or poisoned Balloonist may be shown a player of the same type again. At setup you may add one Outsider.

## General
Each night tell the General which team you honestly think is winning: good, evil, or neither when it is close. The
engine offers its own reading of the Grimoire (deaths, executions, who is on the block); overrule it when you see
the game differently. A drunk or poisoned General is usually told something else; under the Vortox, never your view.

## Preacher
Each night the Preacher picks a player. If it is a Minion, that Minion is woken and told the Preacher chose them,
and has no ability for as long as the Preacher keeps theirs (alive, sober). A player who only thinks they are a good
character (the Marionette) loses the ability without being told. A drunk Preacher does nothing. With the Summoner,
you summon in place of a living Summoner the Preacher silenced.

## Cannibal
The Cannibal has the ability of the most recent player killed by execution. If that player was good, the Cannibal
simply has their ability. If evil, the Cannibal is poisoned until a good player is executed and dies, and you choose
a good ability to pretend they have, usually the one the executed player claimed, so their false information looks
right. Jinxes: a Cannibal who gains the Butler's ability is told; one who ate a Juggler executed on their first day,
having guessed, learns that night how many guesses were right.

## Puzzlemaster
At setup mark one other player, usually a good Townsfolk, as drunk for the whole game, even after the Puzzlemaster
dies (but sober while the Puzzlemaster is drunk or poisoned). Once per game the Puzzlemaster may visit you and name
a player: if they name the drunk, show them the Demon; if not, show them someone who is not the Demon. Never say
whether the guess was right.

## Heretic
While a sober Heretic is in play, alive or dead, the result of the game is reversed at the end: the team that would
win loses, whatever the reason (a Saint's or a Goblin's ending too). The Heretic is never in play with the Spy, the
Baron, the Godfather, the Pit-Hag or the Widow: those jinxes allow only one of each pair.

## Marionette
The Marionette sits beside the Demon and believes they are a good character; treat their information like a
Drunk's, false and consistent. The Demon is told who the Marionette is (only that one of its two neighbours is, while
a Magician lives). Other Minions are not told about them. With the Summoner, the Marionette enters play beside the
summoned Demon instead; with the Kazali, only a neighbour may become the Marionette; with the Plague Doctor, one of the
Demon's good neighbours does. A Marionette who thinks they are the Balloonist may come with an extra Outsider.

## Widow
On the first night the Widow looks at the whole Grimoire and poisons one player for as long as she keeps her
ability. Then you wake one good player, usually a Townsfolk, and show them that a Widow is in play. Jinxes: no
Grimoire while a Poppy Grower has the ability; the Demon's and the Magician's tokens are hidden from her; a Damsel
is poisoned once a Widow has been in play; never with the Heretic.

## Goblin
If the Goblin publicly claims to be the Goblin once nominated (in the defence, aloud or by claiming the ability)
and is executed that day, the Goblin's team wins at once. A claim made before the nomination does not count, nor
does one by a drunk or poisoned Goblin. The Cerenovus may make someone mad that they are the Goblin; with the Plague
Doctor, a Minion may receive the Goblin's ability.

## Kazali
No Minions are dealt: Townsfolk fill their seats, and you may add or remove an Outsider. On the first night the
Kazali points at players and names a Minion for each; they turn evil, learn their new character and the Demon (not
if a Poppy Grower is keeping evil apart). Only a neighbour may be made the Marionette, and it keeps believing it is
good. Never a Minion that cannot share play with the Heretic. After that, the Kazali kills each night.

## Leviathan
Everyone learns at once that the Leviathan is in play. It kills nobody. If a second good player is executed, or day
5 ends with the Leviathan alive, evil wins; killing it wins for good. Jinxes with protection characters reward good:
the Leviathan nominating and executing the Soldier, or the player the Monk, Innkeeper or Exorcist chose last night,
gives good the win; with the Mayor alive on day 5 and no execution, good wins. With the Grandmother, an executed
Grandchild loses for good. With the Banshee, Sage or Ravenkeeper, the Leviathan picks a new living player each night:
a Banshee chosen dies (and gets her powers), a Sage or Ravenkeeper chosen uses their ability without dying. It cannot
be made after day 5 by the Pit-Hag or a tea party.

## Village Idiot
Each night every Village Idiot picks a player and you show their alignment with a thumb. At setup you may turn up
to two more Townsfolk into Village Idiots; with two or more, one of them (your pick) is drunk all game and gets
alignments that may be wrong, consistently. A Spy may show as good and a Recluse as evil. With the Pit-Hag, she may
make another Village Idiot while a token is spare, and you may move the drunk marker; with the Boffin, the Demon may
get the ability even with Village Idiots in play.

## Acrobat
From the second night the Acrobat picks a player. If that player is drunk or poisoned at any point tonight, the
Drunk included and someone who decides to be drunk later in the night (the Organ Grinder), the Acrobat dies. A drunk
or poisoned Acrobat never dies this way.

## Lycanthrope
At setup mark one good player, usually a Townsfolk, who registers as evil to everything while the Lycanthrope keeps
the ability. From the second night the Lycanthrope picks a living player: if that player registers as good they die
and no Demon kill happens tonight; an evil pick does nothing and the Demon kills as usual. A Spy may go either way:
your call.

## Alchemist
At setup pick a Minion on the script that nobody has; the Alchemist holds that ability while staying good, and is
shown it on the first night. Setup effects come with it in a dealt game (the Baron's extra Outsiders, the
Godfather's change). The jinxes reshape some: a Spy, Widow or Marionette chosen this way gives no ability and must be
in play (after each execution an Alchemist-Spy or -Widow may publicly name the Spy or Widow; a right guess makes the
Demon pick that player tonight); a Mastermind gives nothing and stays out of play; an Organ Grinder must also be in
play, and while both are sober both are drunk; a Boffin keeps the Alchemist ignorant of the gift; a Summoner means no
Demon at the start, the Alchemist names the Demon on night three and you name the player, and their death before then
hands evil the win. The engine never asks the Alchemist to choose again.

## Zealot
While five or more players appear alive, a working Zealot's hand goes up on every vote, whatever they intend; they
are reminded each time. A drunk or poisoned Zealot is reminded too, but their vote stands as cast. With the
Cannibal, a Cannibal who gains this ability is told.

## Organ Grinder
Each night the Organ Grinder decides whether to be drunk until dusk. While they are alive and sober, votes happen
with eyes closed: each voter sees only their own hand, nobody hears the count, and the town does not learn who is
about to die until the execution. With the Butler, the Butler may always raise a hand, but it only counts when the
master's is up too.

## Noble
On the first night the Noble sees three players, exactly one of them evil (registration counts: a Spy may be the good
two, a Recluse the evil one). A drunk or poisoned Noble, or one under the Vortox, may see three with no evil player or
with two.

## Farmer
When a sober Farmer dies at night, wake a living good player, usually a Townsfolk, and make them a Farmer. A death by
day passes nothing on. With the Leviathan, the Leviathan picking a good Farmer makes a new Farmer and the old one
survives.

## Harpy
Each night the Harpy picks two players; wake the first and point to the second. Tomorrow the first must try to
convince everyone the second is evil. Just before nominations, if they did not (silence, calling the second good, or
admitting the madness), you may kill the first, the second or both, usually the first. A drunk or poisoned Harpy's
target is still woken, but nobody dies.

## Boffin
On the first night pick a good character nobody has whose ability the engine can run for the Demon, and tell the
Demon and the Boffin. The Demon keeps it while the Boffin lives, and it works whenever the Boffin is sober and
healthy, even while the Demon is drunk or poisoned. The Demon can never have the Drunk's, Heretic's or Politician's
ability; one with the Goon's never turns good through it.

## Boomdandy
If the Boomdandy is executed and dies, sober, everyone but three players dies: usually keep the Demon and two others
alive, so the finish is decided at the table. Then everyone points at a living player after a countdown; the one with
the most fingers dies, and a tie kills nobody. With the Plague Doctor, instead of you gaining it, a player (usually a
living Minion) becomes the Boomdandy.

## Fearmonger
Each night the Fearmonger picks a player; whenever the pick changes, announce to everyone that the Fearmonger has
chosen a player. If the Fearmonger later nominates that player and they are executed, the executed player's team
loses at once. With the Plague Doctor, a living Minion receives this ability and learns it.

## Xaan
X is the number of Outsiders in play; in a dealt game with none, add one. On night X, from the Xaan's step until the
next dusk, every Townsfolk is poisoned, as long as the Xaan lives and is sober. A drunk or poisoned Xaan on night X
does nothing, and the night does not come again.

## Alsaahir
Once a day the Alsaahir may say aloud which players they think are the Minions and which the Demon (each named with a
Minion's or a Demon's character; only the type counts). If the list matches the Grimoire exactly, every Minion and
every Demon, the dead ones included, and nobody else, good wins on the spot. A drunk or poisoned Alsaahir's right list
wins nothing, and a dead one has no ability.

## Huntsman
A real Huntsman brings the Damsel into the game at setup, in an Outsider's seat if one was dealt, otherwise a
Townsfolk's. Once per game, on any night, the Huntsman may point at a living player; whoever they choose, the ability is
spent. If it is the Damsel and the Huntsman is sober, wake her and give her a Townsfolk character nobody has: she stays
on her team. A Marionette who thinks they are the Huntsman comes with a Damsel too.

## Snitch
On the first night wake each Minion who knows they are a Minion and show them three good characters nobody has, their
own bluffs; they need not match the Demon's. A drunk or poisoned Snitch gives the Minions nothing.

## Knight
On the first night show the Knight two other players, neither of whom is the Demon. A Recluse may be shown. A drunk or
poisoned Knight, or one under the Vortox, may be shown the Demon.

## Choirboy
A real Choirboy brings the King into the game at setup, in a Townsfolk's seat. If the Demon's attack kills the King
(a Mayor's bounce onto the King counts), wake the Choirboy that night and point to the Demon. Any other death of the King
tells them nothing; a drunk or poisoned Choirboy may be pointed at anyone.

## King
On the first night wake the Demon and point out the King; a player who becomes a Demon later learns it too. Each night,
once the dead are at least as many as the living, show the King the character of one living player. With the Leviathan
in play, the King learns this from the first death on. A drunk or poisoned King may be shown a character nobody alive
has.

## Cult Leader
Each night the Cult Leader takes the alignment of one of their living neighbours, your choice when the two differ; wake
them only if it changes. By day they may call a cult vote, once a day: everyone, dead or alive, decides with eyes closed
whether to join. If every good player joined, the Cult Leader's team wins at once; otherwise announce only that the vote
is over. A drunk or poisoned Cult Leader changes nothing and wins nothing. A Demon who has this ability through the
Boffin, or an evil player the Pit-Hag made the Cult Leader, never turns good by it.

## Lord of Typhon
One extra Minion is dealt in a Townsfolk's seat, and the evil players sit together in a row with the Lord of Typhon in
the middle, a Minion on each side; you may also add or remove an Outsider, usually removing one or leaving them be.
From the second night it kills like any Demon. With the Summoner, a summoned Lord of Typhon must sit next to a Minion
(pick such a player if the Summoner did not), and its other neighbour becomes an evil Minion.

## Lleech
On the first night the Lleech picks another player as its host: the host is poisoned for the rest of the game while
the Lleech is sober. After that it kills each night. While the host lives, nothing kills a sober Lleech (an execution
fails), except the Assassin; when the host dies, however it happens, the Lleech dies with them. A drunk or poisoned
Lleech can die normally and does not die with its host. Jinxes: a Slayer shooting the host kills the host; with the
Mastermind alive, a host executed leaves the Lleech alive but without its ability; never with the Heretic.

## Bounty Hunter
At setup make one good Townsfolk evil (never a Bounty Hunter): that player learns only that they are evil, and the
other evil players are not told about them. On the first night point the Bounty Hunter at one evil player; whenever
the player they know dies, point them at a different evil player that night. A drunk or poisoned Bounty Hunter may be
pointed at a good player. With the Kazali, wait for its choices: if it made the Bounty Hunter a Minion, nobody is made
evil. A Philosopher who takes this ability may also cost good a Townsfolk, your call.

## Engineer
Once per game, at night, the Engineer names a Demon, or a set of Minions as many as are alive. The living Demon becomes
the one named; for Minions, any Minion already holding a named character keeps it and you share the rest among the
others. Wake each player who changed and show them their new token. Never the Marionette, the Summoner or the Kazali.
A drunk or poisoned Engineer spends it for nothing. With the Summoner, if the living Summoner is turned into another
Minion, you summon the Demon yourself on the third night.

## Princess
If on her first day the Princess nominated the player who was then executed, the Demon still wakes that night but its
attack kills nobody (the Al-Hadikhia's three survive too). She must be alive, sober and healthy at her step. With the
Cannibal, a Cannibal who nominated, executed and killed the Princess today stops the Demon the same way.

## Hermit
The Hermit holds every Outsider ability on the script, whether those Outsiders are in play or not, all at once: a
Saint's loss on execution, a Recluse's misregistration, a Damsel the Minions may hunt, a Tinker's sudden death and so
on. It never holds the Drunk's or the Lunatic's (those are illusions about who you are), nor the Butler's or the
Puzzlemaster's. At setup you may remove one Outsider. Drunk or poisoned, every one of its abilities stops.

## Ogre
On the first night the Ogre picks another player and silently takes that player's alignment, whatever the Ogre's
sobriety; they are not told the result. A Spy always counts as evil here; a Recluse may, and if it does the Ogre is told
they are evil. An evil player the Pit-Hag turns into the Ogre stays evil. A player who gets this ability later picks
at once.

## Psychopath
Each day before nominations the Psychopath may announce a player, who dies on the spot (protections still work); once a
day, and nothing during the nominations. If executed, they play roshambo with whoever nominated them: you decide the
result fairly, as a coin toss; a win means they survive the execution. A drunk or poisoned Psychopath kills nobody and
simply dies when executed.

## Wraith
At the start of each night the Wraith decides whether to open their eyes. Either way they wake with the other evil
players; at dawn tell them who woke and whom they pointed at: everyone if their eyes were open, otherwise only evil
players. Never tell them what anyone was shown. A drunk or poisoned Wraith sees nothing. The Alchemist, the Magician
and the Plague Doctor each have a jinx that brings guesses about the Wraith, or hands its ability to a Minion.

## Vizier
Tell everyone who the Vizier is before the first night (not if the Alsaahir or the Investigator is on the script). By
day the Vizier cannot die, executions included. After any vote in which at least one good player raised a hand, ask the
Vizier whether to execute the nominee at once, whatever the count; doing so ends the day's nominations. A drunk or
poisoned Vizier can die by day and executes nobody, but one silenced by the Courtier or the Preacher is told so and still
cannot die by day. The Magician is immune to it (and loses its own ability), the Fearmonger's pick is off limits and the
Vizier learns it, and a Politician or Zealot voting may count as evil.
