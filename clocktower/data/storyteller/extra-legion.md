# Storyteller's notes: Legion (batch 6, removable)

## Legion
Legion is many players sharing one Demon: most of the table is evil, the good few must find enough allies to carry an
execution, since a vote carried by evil hands alone fails. Your kill each night is the pressure: a good player most
nights, never so fast that the good side has no day left to work with. When only Legion remain, keep killing them until
two are left. Information can still find them, and an Investigator may be shown one of them as a Minion.
