# Storyteller's notes: Riot (batch 6, removable)

## Riot
Riot kills nobody before the third day: the first two days are for the town to find it by talking and executing. On
the third night every living Minion joins it, and the third day's nominations are a chain: each nominee is executed on
the spot and must name the next. The day decides the game. If a nominee has nobody they can name, the chain ends there.
On scripts where it applies, Riot also picks a good player each night (never the same one twice), which matters only for
the few characters whose abilities wait for a death: they get to use them without dying (the Banshee does die).
