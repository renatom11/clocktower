# Storyteller's notes: the Atheist (batch 6, removable)

## Atheist
With the Atheist in play nobody is evil, and your task is to run a believable game anyway: pick a few players as a
pretend evil team before the first night and let the information you give point at them most of the time, so the
town has something to argue about. Mix in truths so it does not read as random. Kill a player most nights, someone
outside your pretend team who is not protected from the Demon, as a real Demon's choice would look. The town wins
only by putting you on the block and executing you; if they never do, the game runs down to two players and they lose.
On a script with the Atheist but without it in play, a nomination of you is the evil team's best trick: executing you
then hands evil the game.
